## Who the game is for
Legend Arrow is a puzzle game for a general audience, including children. We do not ask for anyone's age, name, email or any account. Every player receives our most protective settings (described below).

## What the game itself stores
- **On your device only:** game progress, unlocked creatures, settings (volumes, language, reduced motion, haptics), hint counts and short random receipt codes for rewarded hints. This file stays in the game's private storage. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
- **Local event counters** (for example "level completed", "hint requested") stay on the device and are **never uploaded** in this version.
- We have **no servers**. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts, no cloud saves, no analytics uploads and no notifications.

## Advertising
The game can show an **optional** video ad only when a player chooses "Watch ad · Get 1 hint" after using the three free hints of a puzzle. The whole game can be completed without ads. There are no banners and no ads between levels.

Ads are provided by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To keep players safe:
- All ad requests are marked **child-directed** and **under the age of consent**, with a **G** maximum content rating, so Google limits data use and serves only ads suitable for children and does not use personalised advertising.
- The game does **not** request the Android advertising ID permission (`AD_ID`), and does not use Privacy Sandbox ad APIs.
- Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's **User Messaging Platform** asks for consent before ads are requested. If consent is refused or cannot be checked, the game simply shows no ads; play is never blocked. You can change your choice any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.

When an ad is requested, Google's SDK may process data such as the device's IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad interaction and diagnostic data, under Google's own policies: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>. See the Data Safety inventory for the exact categories [link].

## Permissions
Internet and network state (only to request optional ads and consent information) and vibration (optional haptics, can be switched off). No location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or notifications.

## Children
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no chat, no user profiles and no way to share content. Parents can contact us at [contact email] with any question.

## Your choices
- Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (where applicable).
- Delete all game data: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.
