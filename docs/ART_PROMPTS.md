# Legend Arrow — Art Prompts / Canonical Roster (100 dragons)

Canonical roster: 10 worlds × 10 dragons, world order = difficulty order. Images are owner-generated with ChatGPT (paid plan, commercial use) and saved as `assets/creatures/raw/<dragon_id>.png`.

Color progression by world: 1–2 muted natural · 3–4 vivid natural · 5–7 saturated jewel tones with iridescence · 8–10 spectacular legendary colors with soft glowing accents. UI stays ivory/graphite/slate; only creature art follows this progression.
