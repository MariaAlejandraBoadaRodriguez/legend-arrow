## 1. ENGLISH ONLY

## 2. CAMPAIGN: 10 WORLDS × 10 DRAGONS = 100 PUZZLES
- One puzzle per dragon (100 puzzles), using the worlds and order in docs/ART_PROMPTS.md.
- Difficulty rises steadily: world 1 easy (6–12 arrows) up to world 10 expert (41–60+ arrows, deeper dependency chains, fewer simultaneous exits). Inside each world, levels also get gradually harder.
- Each world gets a restrained atmosphere thumbnail and its English name.
- Completing world N unlocks world N+1. Locked worlds show the silhouettes of their dragons, without pressure or countdowns.
- Every level is generated offline and certified by the existing swept-body solver. No level ships uncertified.
- Large boards must stay playable: zoom/pan when needed, and arrows never shrink below a 48 dp touch target.

## 3. SPEED TIMER + 1–3 STARS
- The timer starts on the first tap. It pauses on the pause menu, overlays, background, ads and the awakening, and survives app kill/relaunch. It is shown discreetly on the board.
- Each level gets a par time derived from its certified solution (arrow count, dependency depth).
  3★ = finished ≤ par with no hints · 2★ = ≤ 2× par (hints allowed) · 1★ = completed.
- Save the best time and best stars per level. Replays can only improve them and never lower them. Rewards and unlocks stay idempotent.
- Results screen: time, personal best ("New record!"), stars appearing one by one (a simple fade with reduced motion), Retry / Next.
- Level select shows stars and best time per level, total stars per world and overall.
- Local personal bests only. No online leaderboard, no account, works offline.

## 4. PHOTOREALISTIC DRAGON ART (owner generates the images; you cannot)
- The owner will place images at assets/creatures/raw/<dragon_id>.png, named as in docs/ART_PROMPTS.md (generated with ChatGPT, paid plan, commercial use).
- Build an import pipeline (scripts/import_art.sh plus a Python tool, pinned dependencies) that, for each image:
  validates the file name against the roster and the size/aspect (≈2:3 portrait, at least 1024 px wide) → removes the background (pinned rembg or an equivalent free tool) → creates a clean transparent cut-out (no gray halo; trim and feather the edges) → creates a thumbnail and a silhouette mask → compresses to WebP within the size budget. It FAILS loudly on a missing, misnamed, wrong-size or badly cut file and prints a per-file report.
- Generate each puzzle's arrow silhouette FROM that dragon's mask, so the board shape matches the real image, then certify it with the solver.
- Awakening for photoreal art: arrows gather into the silhouette → the real image fades in with soft mist, white rim light, subtle breathing/scale, gentle parallax and a short camera push, 4–6 s, skippable after 1 s and replayable from the bestiary. Reduced motion = a short dissolve to the final pose. No duplicate unlock if the awakening is skipped, interrupted, backgrounded or replayed.
- Bestiary detail shows the realistic image with a gentle idle loop. Grid thumbnails stay still.
- Until a dragon's image exists, keep the current art for it and list it as ART PENDING in docs/ART_STATUS.md, with a live count. Never produce a release AAB while any dragon is ART PENDING.
- Record each image's source ("ChatGPT image generation, owner-generated, commercial use") in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
- Produce docs/store/art_review_sheet.png: a contact sheet of every imported cut-out next to its puzzle silhouette, for my visual review.
